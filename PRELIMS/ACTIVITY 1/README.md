# 🔹 Activity 1 — Presentation Design Principles

> **Focus:** Presentation Design

This activity focused on learning and applying the fundamental principles of effective presentation design. It involved organizing information, selecting appropriate colors and fonts, maintaining visual consistency, and creating slides that communicate ideas clearly and effectively.

### What Have I Learned?

Through this activity, I learned that an effective presentation should be visually appealing, organized, and easy to understand. I gained skills in applying proper color combinations, font styles, alignment, and spacing to improve the overall appearance of slides. One challenge I encountered was deciding how to arrange information without making the slides look overcrowded, which I addressed by simplifying the content and prioritizing important details. In the future, I can apply these principles to create more professional, engaging, and informative presentations.
