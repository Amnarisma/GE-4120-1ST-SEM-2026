### 🩵 What Have I Learned?

<div align="justify">
I learned how artificial intelligence can be used as a creative tool to generate images based on specific descriptions and instructions. I developed skills in writing effective prompts, exploring different visual styles, and improving generated outputs by adjusting the details of my instructions. One challenge I encountered was getting the AI to produce an image that closely matched my original idea, which I addressed by making my prompts more specific and descriptive. In the future, I can use AI image-generation tools to support creative projects, presentations, and digital content while ensuring that I use the technology responsibly.
